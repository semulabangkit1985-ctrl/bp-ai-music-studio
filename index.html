how it works

One pipeline, five steps

From raw file to finished master, with a quality-control pass and loudness-matched A/B before anything downloads.

1

Upload

Drop in WAV, FLAC, AIFF or MP3. SONIQ reads sample rate, bit depth and channel layout instantly.

2

Analyze

Loudness, dynamics, frequency spectrum, stereo field and vocal presence, mapped across intro, verse, chorus and drop.

3

Master

The AI decision engine sets EQ, compression, saturation and stereo width — the DSP chain carries it out.

4

A/B

Compare original and master at matched loudness, so nothing sounds better just for being louder.

5

Download

Export mastered WAV and MP3, with the full history saved to your project.
